3 (1) In section 8(2) of the Road Traffic Act 1988, for “50 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath” substitute “31 microgrammes of alcohol in 100 millilitres of breath”.

(2) This regulation applies in relation to cases where the suspected offence is an offence committed in Scotland.
