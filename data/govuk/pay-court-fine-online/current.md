The only service you can use to pay your court fine online, or by telephone using a credit or debit card.

You can pay a court fine or charge online or by phone.

This page is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg).

You can use this service to pay the following in England and Wales:

- court fines
- compensation
- victims surcharge
- prosecutor costs
- criminal courts charge
- confiscation orders
- fixed penalties that have been registered in a magistrates’ court
- vehicle excise back duty

There are different services for paying court fees and parking tickets.

There’s a different way to pay in Northern Ireland and pay in Scotland.

### How to pay

You need your ‘notice of fine’ and a debit or credit card. If you’ve lost your notice of fine, contact the court where you received the fine.

You must pay by the date given in your notice of fine. The court might reject your payment if enforcement action has already started.

If you were fined at a Crown Court, you need your account number and division code.

#### Pay online

You can pay online using the court fines service.

Start now

#### Pay by phone

Telephone: 0300 790 9901 (in England)
 Telephone: 0300 790 9980 (in Wales)
 Find out about call charges

#### Other ways to pay

Contact the Enforcement Office listed on the notice of fine to pay by direct debit, payment card or bank transfer.
