# Independent review: road-traffic test questions and worksheet tool

Reviewer: Claude (did not write the questions or the tool). Reviewed 2026-11-20 against `docs/VERIFY-road-traffic.md`, `data/`, `raw/` (read-only, to see what the pending changes are) and `tools/evaluate/worksheet.py`.
This is legal information, not legal advice. Nothing under `data/` or `raw/`, and not `questions.yaml`, was changed. No question is set to `verified: true`; a person does that.

**How verdicts were chosen.** One verdict per question. If the answer or sources need a definite correction the verdict is `FIX ANSWER` or `FIX SOURCES` (the pending-change caveat is then noted in the reason). `UNSURE` is used where the text seems to support the answer but unapplied changes sit on the provisions the answer relies on and `data/` does not show what they change. Where the answer is wrong *and* sources are missing, I chose `FIX ANSWER` and put the sources in the fix.

### The 5 most serious problems

1. **rt-008 and rt-006 (fixed-penalty default).** The draft answers state things no listed source says. rt-008 says an unpaid fixed penalty is "registered as a fine increased by half": nothing in `data/` says this (s.71 and s.70 only say a "sum payable in default" is registered; s.55, which would say it, is not in `data/`). Both answers say a statutory declaration is available "if you did not receive the notice". s.72(2) of the Road Traffic Offenders Act 1988 (RTOA) allows it only if you were not the person given the notice, or you asked for a hearing in time, and the time limit runs from notice of registration. rt-006 cites MCA 1980 s.14 for the same scenario, but s.14 covers a summons where the court has begun to try the case without the accused knowing. "Respond to the court by the date on the summons" has no source at all. Both are the likeliest to mislead a real user.
2. **rt-003 ("12 within 3 years, minimum 6-month ban").** The only listed source, RTOA s.35, does not contain the 3-year points window (that is s.29(2), in `data/` but not listed, with 1 pending change, s.29(2A)). s.35(2) gives 6 months *only if* no previous disqualification is to be taken into account; otherwise 1 year or 2 years. The draft overstates this as a flat minimum.
3. **Schedule 2 of RTOA 1988 (`ukpga/1988/53/schedule/2`) carries 23 unapplied changes and underpins rt-002, 005, 007, 012, 013, 014, 016, 017.** The pending effects include Crime and Policing Act 2026 s.141(11) and (12) and Automated Vehicles Act 2024 ss.53(2), 54(3), each "words inserted" into Schedule 2 Part 1, plus older uncommenced Road Safety Act 2006 and Criminal Justice Act 2003 amendments. `data/` does not show what words they add, so I cannot rule out an effect on the rows used. Also: Schedule 2 as quoted gives speeding (RTRA s.89(1)) "Level 3 on the standard scale" with no motorway distinction, but the Sentencing Council guideline says "Maximum: Level 3 fine (level 4 if motorway)" and GOV.UK says "£2,500 if you were driving on a motorway". Legislation is meant to win, but the data may be missing the motorway wording. A solicitor must check.
4. **Numbers asserted but not in the listed sources (rt-005, 007, 014, 017).** £1,000 (rt-005), £200 and 6 points (rt-007), and £500 (rt-017) are not in the quoted text of the listed sources. They are available elsewhere in `data/` (Schedule 2, the Fixed Penalty Order, the approach-to-fines guidance, GOV.UK pages) but those units are not listed as expected sources, so the test does not check that search finds the units that carry the figures.
5. **Answers that do not answer the question (rt-021, 022, 030, 032, 016).** rt-021 asks for the penalty and the answer only says it is an offence (the penalty is in s.29(3): level 3 or five times the duty, whichever is greater). rt-022 asks for the limits and the answer only names the Regulations (the table is in reg 2; the name of the Regulations is not in the `data/` text at all, only in `raw/`). rt-030 asks for the speed limit and does not say 30 mph. rt-032 asks about instalments and does not mention them (s.214(8)). rt-016 says only that it is an offence.

Other gaps worth knowing: rt-009 and rt-010 ask about "a road with street lights" but the unit linking street lights to "restricted road" (RTRA s.82) is not in `data/`. rt-033 relies on s.176, which refers to "section 175(2)(a) or (d) or (3)"; s.175 is not in `data/`.

## 3. Tool review: `tools/evaluate/worksheet.py`

**What I ran:** `find_current` for all 49 distinct expected ids in `questions.yaml`, compared with an independent lookup by the `id:` line in each `data/**/current.md`; `render` against a stub index for edge-case questions (no `nation`, empty/null/missing `expected_sources`, missing `expected_answer`, bad nation, unknown id, string instead of list); `split_front_matter` on odd inputs. The checks ran from scratch scripts in the scratchpad folder and wrote nothing into the repo (`git status` still clean).

**Ok:**
- **No wrong or missing matches.** All 49 ids resolve to exactly the file whose front-matter `id` equals the expected id; none render "NOT IN DATA". Collision cases are safe: the glob compares whole directory names, so `section-136` does not match `section-136B` (rt-034 resolves to "Time limit for certain offences", the right unit), `section-5` does not match `section-5A`, and `uksi/2000/2792/schedule` (no trailing slash, so not rewritten) matches `.../2792/schedule/current.md` correctly.
- **Does not alter `questions.yaml`.** The only writes are `args.out.write_text` (line 98). `load_questions` only reads.
- **Truncation of quoted text is declared** ("[... N more lines in <path>]", lines 64-65), and the quotes are otherwise verbatim from `data/` (body only `.strip()`ped, line 24). I spot-checked several against `data/` and they are identical.

**Bugs and weaknesses (file:line, failing input):**
1. `worksheet.py:68` — **"Also returned by search" is silently capped at 6** (`[:6]`) with no "N more" note. rt-018's expected source ranks #28, so at least 27 hits exist; a reviewer sees 6 of them and may conclude nothing else was returned. Failing input: any question with more than 6 non-expected hits (most of them).
2. `worksheet.py:24` and `:59-61` — **crash on non-mapping front matter**. `split_front_matter` returns whatever YAML gives. Input `"---\n\n---\nbody"` returns `(None, 'body')`, and `meta.get(...)` at line 61 raises `AttributeError`. A list front matter returns `(['a','b'], 'body')` (same crash), and invalid YAML (`"---\nid: [\n---\nbody"`) raises `yaml.ParserError`. Fix: `meta = loaded if isinstance(loaded, dict) else {}` and catch `yaml.YAMLError`.
3. `worksheet.py:34-37` — **dead and misleading lookup list.** The glob pattern is `**/<id with / → ->/current.md` searched under `data/<sub>`, but non-legislation ids repeat the folder (`govuk/…`, `sentencing/…`), so the glob can never match them: 9 of 49 ids (all `govuk/*`, `sentencing/*`) resolve only through the fallback at line 39. The fallback rescans every `current.md` (reads the whole file, line 40) per id, which is slow (about 5 s for 49 ids) and masks the primary path being wrong. `"highway-code"` and `"govuk/guidance"` are also not directories under `data/` as written (`data/govuk/guidance/the-highway-code/...`). Behaviour is correct, the code is just not doing what it appears to.
4. `worksheet.py:53` — **`expected_sources` given as a string** (instead of a list) is iterated per character: 23 "NOT IN DATA" blocks for `ukpga/1988/53/section/1`, each triggering a full rescan. `expected_sources: null` and `[]` are handled (empty list renders "also returned" only).
5. `worksheet.py:49-52` — **`q["expected_answer"]` and `q["question"]` raise `KeyError`** if missing (confirmed with a question lacking `expected_answer`). A bad `nation` such as "Scotland UK" raises `ValueError` from `nation_codes` and aborts the whole worksheet rather than flagging the one question.
6. `worksheet.py:60-62` — **reviewer-critical metadata is shown thinly.** `unapplied changes` is a bare count; the worksheet never says what the pending changes are or that `extent_basis: assumed` (e.g. `wsi/2022/800/article/2`, `govuk/seat-belts-law`, `govuk/getting-an-mot`, `govuk/appeal-magistrates-court-decision`) means there is no extent evidence. A missing `title` prints "None". Job A step 4 cannot be done from the worksheet alone for the 7 sources with pending changes (I had to read `raw/`). Suggest printing `extent_evidence` when basis is `assumed` and listing the unapplied effects from the raw XML.
7. `worksheet.py:90` — **default selection hides verified questions** (`not q.get("verified")`); once a person sets `verified: true` the question disappears from the regenerated worksheet, and `--ids` for an unknown id or an unknown `--area` silently produces fewer (or zero) questions with the header "0 questions". `verified: "false"` (a quoted string) would count as verified.
8. `worksheet.py:98` — `--out` is unguarded: `--out tests/road-traffic/questions.yaml` would overwrite the questions file. Not triggered by default, but the "must not alter questions.yaml" guarantee depends on the user.
9. Rank semantics: "Search rank … #28" counts units added by following links, not only search hits; the worksheet header does not say so, which can make a recall problem look like a ranking problem.
